> **Concurrent** computation: multiple tasks can be in progress in any time.

> **[[parallelism|Parallel]]** computation (which is concurrent): multiple tasks cooperate **closely** to solve a problem.

> **Distributed** computation (which is concurrent): a program might need to cooperate with other programs to solve a problem. This cooperation is **looser** then parallelism, as computers are typically connected through the [[Internet]] rather then being connected by design by the hardware itself.
